    The daily round

    Seven chances to save the shift

    Everyone gets the same date-based case. Your doctor starts in the lobby; your patient is waiting on floor 7 in the case’s specialty ward. A correct answer sends the lift directly to floor 7 and saves the patient—even on your seventh guess. Each other answer uses a guess and worsens the patient. Wrong or related answers move the doctor up one floor; a curated same-class answer makes a two-floor express hop. Floor 6 is the last intermediate stop. Seven misses end the round before the patient’s ward opens. There is no real-time countdown.
