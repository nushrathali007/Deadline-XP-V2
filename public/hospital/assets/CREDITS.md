# Model credits

The hospital and Minecraft patient retain the original pixel textures and colours. The upper room now has transparent glass; its seated figure and chair were replaced by a jointed version of the original Steve character and an uploaded bed. One hanging fixture was moved beside the wall to clear the bed. Medical props are simplified and recoloured for browser performance. The live ECG is original application code.

## Lobby and nurse animation update

The room nurse and red/white lobby staff retain the original hospital skins and rigid body pieces. Their legs are segmented for the same eleven-joint convention as the original Steve rig. The original static figure meshes are replaced in place; lobby furniture remains. The pharmacy face is an original canvas label placed over the old sign.

Additional user-supplied animations: Talking.fbx, Bartending.fbx, Female Start Walking.fbx, Sitting (1).fbx, Sitting(1).fbx, Sitting Rubbing Arm.fbx and Sitting Talking.fbx. These are visual motions and do not include voice recordings.

Hospital overview ambience: freesound_community-012174_kwun-tong-hospital-ambience9wav-58596.mp3, supplied by the user. A quiet 23-second loop was prepared from source seconds 28–52, with a baked seam and fade in/out. No additional audio license claim is made.
